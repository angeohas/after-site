Premium & billing

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open After and go to the Premium screen (tap any locked feature), then tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

What's included in Premium, and how much is it?

After is free to download, and most of it stays free: journaling, receipts and standards, no-contact tracking, music detox, letters, and one anchor. Premium adds photo attachments and Dear Future Me letters, the full activities library, unlimited photo clean-out, mood trends and progress insights, AI song suggestions, and up to 3 anchors. Current prices are shown on the Premium screen before you subscribe, and they can vary by country.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose After. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

All payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the After purchase, and choose Request a refund.

Your data & privacy

Where is my journal stored? Can you recover it?

Your journal, voice notes, photos, receipts and anchor details are stored on your iPhone, not on our servers. That keeps them private, but it also means we can't see them or recover them for you.

I got a new phone. Will my data transfer?

Because your data lives on your device, it moves with your iPhone backup. If you set up your new iPhone from an iCloud backup, a computer backup, or Quick Start, your After data should come with it. If you set up the new phone as new, or delete the app, we have no copy we can restore.

How do I delete all my data?

In After, open the Settings tab and tap reset app and start over. This permanently deletes everything stored in the app on that phone. Deleting the app does the same. Feedback or community letters you sent us are stored separately; email us to remove those.

How do I delete a community letter or feedback I sent?

Email after.support@gmail.com and we'll remove it. Letters are anonymous, so tell us roughly what it said and when you sent it.

Using After

What does my anchor see?

Only the message you send them. When you use Contact anchor or I broke contact, After drafts a text and opens your Messages app, and you send it yourself. Your anchor never sees your journal, drafts or check-ins.

How do I change or remove my anchor?

Open the Settings tab. Under your anchor, tap remove next to their name, then add another anchor to pick someone new from your contacts. Free accounts have one anchor; Premium allows up to three.

My notifications aren't showing up.

On your iPhone, open Settings → Notifications → After and turn on Allow Notifications. Also check that a Focus mode (like Sleep or Do Not Disturb) isn't silencing them. After's reminders are scheduled on your phone, so they still work without an internet connection.

I have an idea for a feature.

We'd love to hear it. The fastest way is the feedback box in After's Settings tab, which keeps all ideas in one place for us. You can also email us.
