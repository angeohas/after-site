Privacy Policy for After

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Important note: this policy is split into two parts. Part 1 covers features that exist in the app today. Part 2 covers features that are planned but not yet built (sign-in, analytics, crash reporting, ads). Part 2 is not yet in effect.

Contact us at after.support@gmail.com with any questions.

PART 1 — Current Version

1. Summary

  • Your personal content — journal entries, voice notes, photos, letters, check-ins, receipts, and your anchors' contact details — is stored locally on your device, not on a server we operate.
  • A few optional AI features send a specific slice of data to our server and to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI (Claude), so we can generate a response: talking with After (your messages plus a short summary you control), mood insights (numeric check-in data only), and AI song suggestions (song titles/artists, opt-in only). None of this content is stored on our server after the response is generated. See Section 5 for exactly what's sent. If you send us feedback, that text is stored so we can read it (Section 5).
  • After, the AI in the "remind me" tab, is an AI, not a person or a therapist. It is part of Premium, only works after you agree on a consent screen, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not currently use advertising networks or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on which features you use, you may enter: your name and pronouns; one or more anchors' names and phone numbers (people notified if you reach out to your ex or break no-contact — you may select these from your device Contacts); your ex's name and receipts & standards entries; journal content including written entries, saved-but-unsent drafts, voice recordings, photos, and letters to your future self; an optional personal photo; photos and playlists you choose to review or remove in the clean-out section (including connecting your Spotify account); daily check-in data (mood, sleep, eating, drinking, outside-time); your conversations with After and messages you hold in Drafts; your loose ends checklist; your progress/mood entries; and any feedback text you choose to send us.

You can delete any of this information at any time within the App, or remove all of it by uninstalling the App. Feedback you've already sent to us is retained on our server as described in Section 5; contact us to request its deletion.

4. How Your Information Is Stored

The content described in Section 2 — your journal, voice notes, photos, letters, anchor information, receipts, check-in data, and your conversations with After — is stored locally on your device. We cannot see, access, back up, or recover it, because it never reaches us in the first place, with the narrow exceptions in Section 5. Deleting the App permanently deletes all locally stored After data.

5. Data We Do Receive: AI Features, Feedback, and Community Letters

We operate a lightweight backend server for a small number of specific features. It does not have a database of your personal content — the only things it ever receives, and what happens to each, are described below.

Talking with After (the "remind me" tab). After is an AI, available with a Premium subscription. Before your first conversation, the App shows a consent screen explaining what is sent, and After only works if you agree. When you send a message, the App sends the following to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to write a reply:

  • your message and the recent conversation (up to your last 40 messages and After's replies), so After can follow along;
  • a short summary built from things you've saved in the App, such as your name and pronouns, your ex's name, your anchor's first name, your day count and no-contact streak, recent receipts and standards, your "i am" list, recent check-in moods, and how many times this week you wrote to your ex without sending. You can see every item, and switch any of them off, on the "what After remembers" page. Items you switch off are never sent. Your full journal, voice notes, photos, letters, and anchors' phone numbers are never sent;
  • your local date and time, so After knows if it's the middle of the night;
  • an anonymous app user ID created by our subscription provider (not your name, email, or Apple ID), used to check with our subscription provider whether you have Premium and to apply usage limits that protect the service from abuse.

We do not store or log the content of your conversations on our server. The only thing we keep is a count of how many messages each anonymous app user ID sent on each day, which is used to prevent abuse of the service. Your conversation is saved only on your device, and you can clear it at any time. To recognize messages that might mean someone is in danger, both the App and our server check the text of your latest message for certain phrases; if one matches, the App shows crisis resources such as 988. This check happens in the moment and its result is not stored. After can make mistakes and is not a crisis service.

Mood insights. If you're a Premium subscriber, the Progress tab automatically generates a short written observation about your recent patterns. To do this, we send your check-in data — mood scores as plain numbers (0–4), your no-contact streak, days using the app, check-in count, and days marked no-contact or relapsed — to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the observation. No journal text, voice notes, or anything you've written is included. This request is not logged or stored on our server; it exists only for the moment it takes to generate your insight.

AI song suggestions. In the music detox section, you can optionally enable AI-generated suggestions for songs similar to ones you're avoiding. This is off by default and only turns on if you explicitly opt in, after being shown what it sends. When you request suggestions, the titles and artists of the songs you've quarantined or marked "healing" (not your journal, mood, or anything else) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate similar suggestions. This request is not logged or stored on our server.

Feedback. If you use the in-app feedback box, the text you write is sent to and stored on our server so we can read it. Unlike the two features above, feedback text is retained (not just transiently processed) until we act on it or you ask us to delete it. Don't include anything in feedback you wouldn't want stored — it's the one place in the App where written text is not kept only on your device.

Community letters. In "from a friend," you can optionally write an anonymous letter for other users. If you submit one, the text you write (and the "for when…" line you choose) is sent to and stored on our server, where we review it before deciding whether to publish it to other users. We don't collect your name or an account with it, and we may edit or remove a letter at any time. Please don't include names, contact details, or anything identifying. We can't reply to submissions. Approved letters are shown to all users, and anyone can report a letter from within the App. You can ask us to remove a letter you submitted by emailing after.support@gmail.com.

All three AI features call Anthropic's Claude API to generate their responses; Anthropic processes those requests as our service provider under its own privacy policy and commercial terms. These requests are rate-limited per IP address; your IP address is used only for that rate limit and is not stored.

6. Sharing With Your Anchor, and Spotify

The "Contact anchor" and "I broke contact" features draft a text message and open your device's native Messages app, addressed to your anchor's phone number. This message is sent directly from your device through your own carrier — it does not pass through, or get logged by, any server operated by After. By entering someone's contact information as your anchor, you confirm you have their permission to store their information for this purpose and to message them through the App.

If you connect your Spotify account, After uses Spotify's official authentication to access playlist information you authorize. We do not store your Spotify credentials — authentication is handled by Spotify directly. Your use of Spotify through After is also subject to Spotify's Privacy Policy.

7. Subscriptions & Purchases

Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store and your Apple ID. After does not receive or store your payment card details. We use a subscription-management provider to manage subscription status and entitlements, which may receive a device identifier and purchase/subscription status (not payment details). When you talk with After, our server uses your anonymous app user ID to ask this provider whether you have Premium, so it can apply the right message limit.

8. Data Security

Because almost all of your personal content is stored locally rather than transmitted to us, protecting your device (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID) is the primary safeguard for your data. For the limited data described in Section 5, we rely on standard transport encryption (HTTPS) and our infrastructure provider's security practices; the AI requests themselves are not stored by us, and feedback text is stored on infrastructure we control and do not share with third parties beyond what's needed to host it.

9. Your Choices & Rights

You can access or review your data by opening the relevant section in the App, delete specific entries using the delete controls within each section, or delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App. For After, you can clear your conversation, switch off what After remembers, or turn After off entirely from the "remind me" tab or Settings. To request deletion of feedback you've sent us, email after.support@gmail.com. If you're located somewhere with data protection laws (EU/UK, California, etc.) and have questions about how these rights apply, contact the same address.

10. Age Requirement

After is rated 16+ and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the required age.

11. Not a Substitute for Professional Care

After is a self-guided personal tool, not a medical, psychological, or crisis service, and cannot respond in an emergency. After, the AI, is not a therapist or a person, and what it says may be wrong or incomplete. If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact a crisis line (in the US: call or text 988) or emergency services in your area.

PART 2 — Planned Additions (not yet live)

The following are planned but not yet built: account sign-in (with Sign in with Apple offered alongside any third-party option), analytics, crash reporting, and advertising. This Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect each of these before they become active in the App.
