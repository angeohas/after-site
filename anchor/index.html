be their anchor 🌙

A friend is using i miss me to get through a breakup, and they asked you to be their anchor: the person who gets a heads-up when they might need you.

What you'll get: a text from i miss me when your friend could use a check-in (like when they're about to text their ex), a heads-up when they lock their apps before going out, and the code to unlock them early if they really need it. You can reply, and your message goes to them privately in the App.

how to say yes

  1. Tap the button below. Your Messages app opens with a text to i miss me already written for you.
  2. Hit send.
  3. i miss me texts you back. Reply YES, and you're their anchor.

private by design

  • Every invite link has its own random code. It isn't based on anyone's name, so it can't be guessed.
  • Each code works once and expires after 7 days.
  • Your friend approves you in the i miss me app before you get any updates about them. Nobody becomes their anchor without their yes.
  • You can reply STOP at any time, and your friend can remove you from the app whenever they want.
