after

a quiet place to heal after a breakup.

After gives you somewhere to put the text you want to send at 2am, a friend who's quietly in your corner, and your own reasons it ended, for when your heart tries to rewrite them.

Download on the App Store Free · Premium optional

built for the hardest hours

For the parts of a breakup that are hardest to face alone.

Not just a journal. A toolkit for the 2am urges, the songs that ambush you at the grocery store, and the doubt that tries to tell you it wasn't that bad.

No contact, tracked honestly

Gentle daily check-ins and a streak that counts the days you didn't reach out. Broke contact? No shame. It's day 1 again, and After helps you through it.

Songs and photos, on your terms

Quarantine the songs that hit too hard, then move them to healing when you're ready. Sort your camera roll one photo at a time: keep it, archive it privately, or let it go.

Something that's just for you

A goals list that has nothing to do with them, and a scrapbook that fills up as you check things off. Healing is also finding your way back to yourself.

the toolkit

Everything has one job: getting you through tonight.

The draft interceptorWrite the text you want to send. After shows you one of your own receipts before you decide.
Your anchorSomeone you choose who gets a quiet heads-up if you break no contact. They never see your journal.
Receipts & standardsThe real reasons it ended, in your own words, so 2am doubt can't rewrite your history.
The night wallOpen After between midnight and 4am and you're met with slow breathing and a way through the hour.
Dear future meSeal letters to yourself for 30, 60 or 90 days, or for whenever you need them.
Letters from a friendWords for the hardest moments, from people who've been through it too.

private by design

Your journal stays on your phone.

Your entries, voice notes, photos, receipts and your anchor's number are stored on your device, not on our servers. We don't sell your data, and we don't use advertising networks or third-party analytics.
